Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This file describes the open-source / community build of LM Mini. The App Store and Google Play apps are covered at lmmini.com/privacy.html.

Chats stay on your devices

Conversations, settings, and server details are stored locally. Uninstalling the app removes that data.

Chat sync never stays on our servers

If you use LM Mini Home chat sync, traffic is encrypted and relayed in real time. We do not store your chats on the relay.

This build

The GitHub stub does not use LM Mini’s production Firebase project or Pro cloud backup.
