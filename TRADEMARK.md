# LM Mini trademark policy

The MIT license in [LICENSE](LICENSE) covers the code in this repository. It
does not cover the LM Mini name or the LM Mini app icon.

The LM Mini name and app icon are not licensed for forks. Before you publish a
build of this code (in an app store, as a download or in any other way),
rename it:

- use your own app name,
- use your own bundle id / application id,
- replace the app icon.

You may say that your app is "based on the LM Mini source code", as long as it
does not suggest that your build is the official LM Mini app or is endorsed by
its authors.
